Win chance = how often SportsEdge thinks the bet wins. Edge = how much higher that is than DraftKings' price requires. Bigger edge, better bet.

Alternate lines worth taking at

If your book's alternate line menu offers one of these at or better, the model says it's a +EV bet. "Lay up to −3" means −3, −2.5, −2 and so on all qualify. The app doesn't pull alternate lines itself, so check these against your book.
